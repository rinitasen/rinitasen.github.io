How I Work

The Problem I Solve

Your product is solid. Your customers love it. But your content isn’t converting your prospects into buyers.

The copy you may already have sounds generic. The messaging could belong to any competitor. Visitors land on your website and your social media channels and leave without taking action.

You know your content needs work. But you don’t have the strategy or the consistent execution to fix it.

That's where I come in.

My Process

How I Work With You

1

Understand Your Business and Audience Deeply

Before writing a single word, I invest time in understanding your product architecture, your competitive landscape, and who your buyers are and what they care about.

2

Research What Messaging Resonates With Your Buyers

I bring the same rigour I applied to competitive intelligence projects at Genpact and Frost & Sullivan, that is, understanding what your market is saying, where your competitors are weak, and what your buyers are waiting to hear.

3

Write Content That Moves People Toward Conversion

Every asset, whether a long-form blog post, a case study, or a landing page, is written with a specific buyer, a specific moment in their journey, and a specific action in mind.

4

Refine Based on What's Working

I track performance, read the signals, and refine what is and isn't working, the same way I built and iterated dashboards for clients in Looker Studio.
