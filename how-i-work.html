How I Work

The Problem I Solve

Your product is solid. Your customers love it. But your content isn't converting your prospects into buyers. The copy sounds generic. The messaging could belong to any competitor. Visitors land and leave without taking action. That's where I come in.

My Process

How I Work With You

1

Understand Your Business and Audience Deeply

Before writing a single word, I invest time in understanding your product architecture, your competitive landscape, and exactly who your buyers are and what they care about.

2

Research What Messaging Resonates With Your Buyers

I bring the same rigour I applied to competitive intelligence projects at Genpact and Frost & Sullivan — understanding what your market is saying, where your competitors are weak, and what your buyers actually want to hear.

3

Write Content That Moves People Toward Conversion

Every asset — whether a long-form blog post, a case study, or a landing page — is written with a specific buyer, a specific moment in their journey, and a specific action in mind.

4

Refine Based on What's Working

Content strategy is not a one-and-done exercise. I track performance, read the signals, and refine what isn't working — the same way I built and iterated dashboards for clients in Looker Studio.
